Lifetime prediction and the economic lifetime of Proton Exchange
Membrane fuel cells

Huicui Chen, Pucheng Pei , Mancun Song

State Key Lab. of Automotive Safety and Energy, Tsinghua University, Beijing 100084, China

highlights

 A formula to predict the PEMFC lifetime is presented.
 A PEMFC residual life evaluation method is presented.
 The evaluation method realizes online forecasting of the PEMFC residual life.
 The PEMFC economic lifetime is studied to confirm the best design lifetime.
