highlights

 Flexible Simulink model with variable operating conditions.
 Water transfer across the membrane is considerable, about 10% of total incoming water.
 Mathematical and Simulink model validated using available commercial electrolyzer data.
 Balance of system accomplished using simple electronics and coding.
